Frequently Asked Questions

What is plagiarism?

Plagiarism is presenting another person's words or ideas as your own without appropriate acknowledgement.

Does a similarity score prove plagiarism?

No. A similarity score indicates potentially matching content. The sources and context should always be reviewed.
